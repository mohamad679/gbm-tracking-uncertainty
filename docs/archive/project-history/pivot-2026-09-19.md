# Decision record: reference-backed, human-independent evaluation

Date: 2026-09-19. Decision: **GO with revised scope.**

## Reason

The two planned independent reviewers are no longer available. Machine or LLM inspection cannot replace independent human ground truth. Waiting for new reviewers would make the project schedule depend on an unavailable resource, while treating machine-generated tracks as truth would invalidate the central uncertainty question.

## What is retained

- Source/data audits and verified provenance.
- Two real GlioTrace ROI samples and their private derived viewer.
- Crop, border, axis and metadata diagnostics.
- Deterministic machine track proposals, explicitly labeled unverified.
- U373 archive audit and reference-track parser.

## What changes

- U373, followed by T98G if its schema passes audit, supplies existing expert reference annotations.
- Seeded corruptions create known missed detections, localization errors, fragments, ID switches, false positives and wrong links.
- Tracking metrics and downstream sensitivity are computed against those known references.
- GlioTrace becomes an unlabeled qualitative generalization case study.
- Image-derived states are called latent or morphological states, not validated phenotypes.

## Primary question

How do known tracking errors propagate into migration summaries and latent-state dynamics, and does calibrated uncertainty reduce error or support reliable abstention on held-out sequences?

## Claims explicitly excluded

- GlioTrace machine tracks are accurate without reference labels.
- Image-derived states are validated biological phenotypes.
- U373 or T98G performance proves brain-slice invasion accuracy.
- Two mice or a few ROIs support treatment-effect inference.

## Immediate gate

Freeze U373 sequence-level development/test roles; reproduce reference identities, consecutive links, centroids and lineage intervals; save provenance and deterministic checks. Do not implement HMM, SLDS or Koopman models before controlled corruption and baseline metric gates pass.
