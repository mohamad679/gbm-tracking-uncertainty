# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes technical discussion and contributions conducted professionally and respectfully.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss code, evidence, and design decisions rather than attacking people.
- Give specific, reproducible feedback when reporting defects or scientific concerns.
- Distinguish measured results from interpretation and speculation.
- Respect dataset licenses, privacy constraints, and research boundaries.
- Do not misrepresent this technical benchmark as clinical or biological validation.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate disclosure of private information, spam, or knowingly misleading scientific claims are not acceptable in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to protect the project and its contributors. Security-sensitive disclosures should follow `SECURITY.md` rather than public issue threads.
